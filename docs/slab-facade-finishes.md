# Storey boundaries, floors and ceilings

The intermediate floor zone is part of the wall facade. `buildStoreyGeometry` uses the prepared wall surfaces and each storey's footprint to give each perimeter segment exactly one owner. A matching lower exterior wall continues upwards; where there is no matching lower exterior face, a matching upper wall continues downwards. Matching requires an outward-facing surface on the actual footprint boundary. A coplanar internal partition inside a lower extension cannot carry its interior finish into the exterior zone above it. Ownership splits at footprint corners even when a source face spans the corner. Any unsupported edge remains an edge of the horizontal assembly.

These continuations enter the wall renderer before roof clipping. They retain the source face's material, pick identity and metre-based UV frame. Roof partitions therefore divide the wall and its floor zone together, including continuations below an upper wall's nominal base. They do not use the former independent slab-edge material lookup or asynchronous completed-face store. Ordinary clicks select the same surface above and below the room-height line; Shift-selection still follows connected exterior surfaces and excludes regions hidden by roofs.

Horizontal assemblies retain independent top surfaces, undersides and stair-opening reveals. Top and bottom surfaces are cut at their own elevations: a sloping roof's intersection with the top must not remove too much underside. New edges introduced by roof cutouts do not become exposed vertical slab fascias. Existing `floor-slab-edge` material assignments apply to unsupported edges and opening reveals; wall finishes own the facade continuations.

An intermediate assembly exists only between storeys and uses the lower room's ceiling height and the next storey's actual elevation. No assembly is added above a bungalow or the highest storey. Cutaway views generate continuations only for the intermediate floors actually displayed.

The UI calls the existing `slabThickness` value **Intermediate floor depth**. The saved field is retained for compatibility; it describes an assembly depth, not a particular construction material. Changing it still moves the storeys above. Roof assemblies continue using their own roof-thickness setting.

The highest storey also offers **Ceiling under roof**: **Horizontal ceiling** or **Open to roof**. `ceilingMode` is optional and defaults to horizontal for existing projects. Opening it removes both the horizontal ceiling finish and its pick surfaces. If another storey is added above, its intermediate floor retains a ceiling; the stored roof-ceiling preference applies again if that storey becomes the highest.

Geometry preparation is cached independently of material and selection edits; roof clipping stays in workers. Tests cover boundary ownership, UVs, partial support, unsupported edges, top-storey and cutaway behaviour, roof clipping, and independent horizontal caps. Browser regressions check facade selection/painting in `roof_tests.json`, roof material regions in both saved projects, top-storey ceiling changes, and responsiveness.
