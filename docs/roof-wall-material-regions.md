Walls abutted by a roof from a lower floor keep their solid geometry. Their exterior material surfaces are divided at the roof contact profile, so disconnected exposed areas can be selected and finished independently.

Normal clicks select a contiguous panel or roof region. Shift-click selects the adjoining exposed exterior surfaces, including corner returns and other exposed roof regions; wall regions behind roofs are excluded.

Coplanarity describes the wall plane; roof regions also constrain material selection. Connected wall pieces beneath the panels of the same roof share one selection region, including the wall around a doorway. A roof ridge must not split that selection into left and right halves. Exposed areas above the sloping roof contact remain separate. Panel-specific face IDs stay unchanged so existing saved finishes remain valid.

The division uses the structural roof profile through adjoining roof shells, avoiding a narrow selection bridge beneath the upper eaves. Only faces towards the roof's local support footprint receive those boundaries. Junction panels can extend through the wall to meet another roof; that extension must not divide the opposite, room-facing finish. A normal click on that side selects the continuous coplanar wall around openings, including areas above and below the exterior roof lines. Existing facade finishes are inherited; assignments to a new roof region cannot fall back onto neighbouring regions. UV coordinates are interpolated across each division.

Surface division runs in the wall clipping worker. `wallRoofSurfacePartitions.test.ts` covers the saved `roof_tests.json` junction, area preservation, UVs, worker transport and roof reordering. In `tests/browser/roofWallRegression.html?roof-tests`, run `checkRoofMaterialRegions` from `roofMaterialRegionsRegression.js` to check clicks, shift-selection and painting one exposed region independently.

Contact detection is independent of the whole-wall abutment exemption used for geometry clipping. This also handles roofs meeting only part of a longer facade and slightly angled Bay mounting edges in `red_house_3.json`. Room surface faces retain their existing selections. Run `checkRedHouseRoofMaterialRegions` on the same harness with `?materials` to check the saved house's main roof and Bay contacts.

On that harness, `checkRoomSideOfRoofDoorway` and `checkRoofDoorwayRegions` from `roofDoorwayRegionsRegression.js` check both sides of the doorway wall: the room side selects and paints continuously, while the roof-facing side retains separate exposed finishes.

Vertical closures created by roof cuts inherit the touching coplanar wall side, including its UV frame. They remain selectable and connect the facade on either side of a cut wall junction. Exposed faces above a roof are classified using that roof region rather than the room footprint below them. Door and window reveals join selection on their own wall side when their edges touch that selected surface; the reverse reveals keep their separate finish. `checkRoofJunctionFinishes` in `roofJunctionFinishesRegression.js` checks the saved house's junction strip and doorway painting.

Soffits are clipped as complete boxes against adjoining roof spaces after being generated from the trimmed roof panels. This removes the portions projecting into a vaulted room while retaining exterior eaves. `roofEavesClipping.test.ts` covers that intersection in the saved house.

Shift-selection classifies an exposed roof region as exterior only where the room-facing sample lies within the support footprint of the roof that created that region. Extended junction panels must not turn part of an interior window wall into an exterior selection. Covered fragments with an adjacent room also remain in that room's selection. `wallRoomRoofSelection.test.ts` covers the classification; `checkRoomRoofShiftSelection` in `tests/browser/roomRoofShiftSelectionRegression.js` checks Shift-clicking either side of Red House's upstairs front window and painting the whole room without changing the exterior face.
